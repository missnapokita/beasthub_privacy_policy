Privacy Policy

Beasthub – Guide & Wallpaper
Effective date: September 29, 2026

Beasthub ("we," "our," or "the app") provides hero guides, skin images, downloadable content, and wallpaper features. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the app and the choices available to you.

Information Collection And Use

You can browse guides and wallpapers without creating an account. The app stores some information on your device, including selected settings, cached content, download history, and any folder access you choose to grant. Your device model and Android version may be shown in the Edit Access screen to help explain available methods. We do not use these local records to identify you personally or sell them.

The app connects to online content providers to load catalogs, guides, images, tutorials, updates, and requested downloads. Like other internet services, these providers may receive connection information such as your IP address and request details.

Download Feature And Optional File Access

When you tap Download, the app retrieves the selected content from its online source. Temporary download files are processed on your device and removed after the operation. A record of the selected item may remain in the app's local History until you clear it or the app's data.

If you choose a feature that applies downloaded content, the app may ask you to select a folder using Android's file picker or authorize Shizuku. This access is used to copy or extract the content you selected into the authorized location. The app does not require this access merely to browse guides or view wallpapers. You can decline or revoke access in Android or Shizuku settings. Files already applied outside the app's storage may remain there until you remove or restore them.

Wallpaper Feature

You can preview and crop a skin image, then choose to apply it to your home screen, lock screen, or both. The app downloads the selected image and uses Android's wallpaper service when you tap an apply option. The app does not upload your personal photos for this feature. You can change your wallpaper later in Android settings.

Advertising And Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads and rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID where available) for ads, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners handle this information under their own policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

The app also loads content from GitHub and other image or content hosts. Opening a tutorial or other external link takes you to a separate service, which may have its own privacy policy. Shizuku is a separate app and has its own data practices.

Data Storage And Security

App settings, history, and cached content are kept on your device; your Android backup settings may apply. You can clear History within the app where available, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove app-managed local information. This does not automatically delete content you applied in another app's folder or a wallpaper already set on your device. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies.

We rely on Android's app storage and permission controls. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children'S Privacy

The app is not designed specifically for children under 13. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern may contact us using the details below.

Changes To This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will show the latest version.
