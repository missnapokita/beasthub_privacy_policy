Privacy Policy

Beasthub – Guide & Wallpaper (package: com.beasthub.ultra)
Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Beasthub ("we," "us," or "the app") accesses and handles information when you browse hero guides and skins, download content, use storage access features, view advertisements, or set a wallpaper.

Information the app handles

  • Local settings and history: The app stores your selected access method, granted folder reference, download and restore history, and cached catalog or guide data on your device. It may show your device model and Android version on the Edit Access screen. These settings and history are not used to create an account.
  • Files and folder access: If you choose to download or apply content, the app downloads the selected file and may extract or copy it to the folder you authorize. On supported devices, it uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF), legacy storage permissions, or Shizuku, according to your selected method. The app uses this access to perform your requested action. Downloaded temporary ZIP files are removed after processing; applied files may remain in the destination until you remove or restore them.
  • Wallpaper images: When you choose a skin image and tap a wallpaper action, the app fetches and processes that image on your device, then asks Android to set it as your home or lock screen wallpaper. The app does not ask you to upload your personal photos.
  • Network requests: The app retrieves catalogs, hero guides, tutorial or update configuration, images, and selected downloads from online sources such as GitHub and other content hosts. Those services may receive standard connection data, such as your IP address, when a request is made.

Advertising

Beasthub uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads for regular content and rewarded ads for certain VIP content. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address, app interactions (such as launches, taps, and ad views), diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. Google uses these for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. We do not receive your advertising ID as an app account identifier.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ad choices and consent options may vary by region.

Permissions and your choices

  • Internet and network state: Used to load app content, images, downloads, updates, and ads.
  • Folder or storage access: Used only when you select an access method and authorize a folder or grant the relevant permission. You can revoke folder access in Android settings, and you may use the app's guide and download-only features without applying files.
  • Shizuku access: If you select Shizuku, its separate permission flow allows the app to carry out the requested file operation. You can revoke that permission in Shizuku or Android settings.
  • Set wallpaper: Used only when you choose to apply a wallpaper.
  • Advertising ID: May be used by Google Mobile Ads as described above. Android settings provide controls for this ID.

Sharing and outside services

We do not sell your personal files, download history, or folder contents. Google and its advertising partners may process ad-related data through AdMob. Catalog and image hosts receive requests needed to deliver their content. If you open a tutorial, update, or other external link, your browser or the destination service handles information under its own privacy policy. Shizuku is a separate app with its own data practices.

Storage, security, and deletion

History, preferences, and cached content are stored on your device; Android backup settings may also apply. You can clear the app's history where available or clear its storage or uninstall it to remove app-managed local data. Uninstalling does not necessarily remove files you previously applied to another folder or wallpapers you set through Android. Third-party services, including Google, retain and delete data according to their own policies.

We use Android's permission controls and app storage for local data. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Online content is fetched from the URL provided by its host; avoid granting folder access to content you do not trust. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Children

Beasthub is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy concern, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, advertising, or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised version is posted.
